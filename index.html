Alarms support

Alarms is an alarm for people who switch off ordinary alarms in their sleep. It rings as a system alarm through Silent mode and Focus, and offers missions: math problems, shaking the phone, steps or a QR code scan. It requires iOS 26.0 or later. Math and shake can be completed without getting out of bed.

My alarm did not ring

Alarms needs your permission to schedule alarms. Open the Settings app, choose Alarms and make sure it is allowed to schedule alarms. With that permission, alarms ring with the app closed, through Silent mode and Focus.

What happens after Stop?

Stop silences the current signal. If you have not completed a mission, another system alarm is scheduled for 60 seconds later. Each Stop repeats this process until a mission is completed; there is no configurable repeat limit.

A mission will not finish

After three failed attempts, or when a sensor is not available, the mission screen offers an emergency exit. You must start it explicitly; it ends the alarm after a 60-second countdown.

Subscription

Alarms is free with one alarm, the math and shake missions, all twelve sounds and the last seven days of your morning log. Alarms Premium adds unlimited alarms, the steps and QR missions, your whole morning history and the wake-up check. It is a monthly or yearly auto-renewing subscription; the yearly plan starts with a free trial.

To cancel, open the Settings app, tap your name, then Subscriptions, and choose Alarms Premium. Cancel at least 24 hours before the end of the current period to avoid the next charge.

Usage statistics

In build 10 and later, analytics is optional: the first-launch sheet has an unchecked Optional — Share usage data checkbox and a separate Required — Terms of Use checkbox. Terms alone enables Continue, and the app and Premium work without analytics. AppMetrica starts only when sharing is explicitly enabled; users updating from the older single-button agreement see the new unchecked choices. The sheet links to the Terms and Privacy Policy. When enabled, AppMetrica processes usage events, technical identifiers, IP-derived region and purchase metadata when available from StoreKit. The app does not include alarm labels, registered QR payloads or morning-log contents in analytics events. To stop SDK data sending, open Settings in the app and turn off Share usage data under Analytics; the privacy policy explains what is sent, how long it is kept and how to request deletion.

Restore purchases

On a new iPhone, open Alarms, go to Settings and tap Restore Purchases while signed in with the Apple Account you subscribed with.
