Privacy Policy

Effective September 22, 2026

This policy explains how Alarms, an alarm app for iPhone published by TSApps, handles your information. The short version: your alarms and your morning log stay on your iPhone, and the only thing that leaves it is anonymous usage statistics you can switch off.

What Alarms collects

One thing: anonymous usage statistics, so we can see which parts of the app people use and where it gets in the way. Alarms has no account and no advertising, and it does not track you across other apps or websites. Everything you create in the app stays on your iPhone.

Usage statistics

The statistics are collected with AppMetrica, a mobile analytics service operated by Yandex, which processes them on our behalf. Each report carries the name of something that happened in the app — an alarm was created, a mission was started or finished, the fallback ended an alarm, the paywall was shown, a subscription was bought — together with the app version, the device model, the iOS version, an identifier AppMetrica generates for this app on this device, and the IP address the request arrives with, from which a rough region is derived.

What is never sent: your alarm times and labels, the text of a QR code you registered, your morning log, and anything else you type. The identifier is not your Apple Account and is not linked to your name, and we never receive a way to contact you.

You can switch the statistics off: open Alarms, go to Settings and turn off Share usage data under Analytics. Nothing is sent after that. Yandex describes how it processes the data in its privacy policy.

What is stored on your device

Your alarms (time, repeat days, label, sound, missions and the text of a QR code you register), your morning log (when an alarm rang, when you finished and which mission you did) and your settings are stored only on your iPhone, inside Alarms. The widget reads your next alarm from storage shared with the app on the same device. Deleting Alarms deletes all of it. If you back up your iPhone, Apple's backup includes this data the way it includes any app's; we cannot see it.

Purchases and subscriptions

Alarms Premium is sold through the App Store. Apple processes the payment; we never see your payment details or your Apple Account. The app only asks the App Store whether your subscription is active.

Permissions

Alarms asks for a permission only when a feature needs it, and you can change each answer in the Settings app:

Alarms let Alarms ring as a system alarm through Silent mode and Focus with the app closed.

Motion & Fitness is used while a shake or steps mission runs, to count shakes and steps on the iPhone. The readings are not stored.

Camera is used to scan the QR code you register and, during the QR mission, to scan it again. No image is saved or sent.

Notifications are used only for the wake-up check, a reminder five minutes after a mission, if you switch it on.

Children

Alarms is not directed at children under 13, and it collects no information from anyone, children included.

Changes to this policy
