Privacy Policy

Effective October 6, 2026

This policy explains how Alarms, an iPhone alarm app published by TSApps, handles information. Your alarm plans, registered QR payloads and morning log are stored on your device. In build 10 and later, AppMetrica starts only if you select the optional Share usage data checkbox and tap Continue, or subsequently enable sharing in Settings. Accepting the Terms of Use alone does not enable analytics. You can stop sharing usage data in the app’s Settings. Before a separate analytics choice the SDK is not activated.

What Alarms collects

We use AppMetrica, operated by Yandex, to understand app usage and improve Alarms. The app reports events such as first acceptance during a fresh installation’s first session, saving and successfully scheduling an alarm, observing a system alarm alert, starting or completing a mission, using the emergency exit, viewing the paywall, purchasing or restoring Premium, and changing the wake-up check setting. Event parameters include mission type, difficulty, attempt count, product identifier and setting state.

Session and technical data also supports the SDK's operation, including managing sessions and requests. The SDK also processes session and technical information, including app and SDK versions, device model, operating system, language, technical device identifiers such as an AppMetrica-generated identifier and the identifier for vendor (IDFV), and the IP address used to connect. An approximate region can be derived from the IP address. This information is not fully anonymous: identifiers can distinguish an installation or device over time. Alarms has no account or advertising and does not use the advertising identifier (IDFA) or an advertising support module.

AppMetrica's automatic purchase tracking is enabled in the SDK configuration. When the SDK receives purchase information from StoreKit, it can send purchase and subscription metadata, such as product and transaction identifiers, price, currency and receipt information. The app also reports the product identifier when a purchase completes. Apple processes payments; Alarms does not receive your payment card details or Apple Account credentials.

Alarm times and labels, registered QR payloads, the contents of your morning log, camera images and motion readings are not included in the app's analytics events. Alarms does not request location permission or supply a location to AppMetrica, so it does not enable GPS collection through a location permission request.

Agreement and sharing controls

Analytics is optional and is off until you make a separate choice to enable it. In build 10 and later, the first-launch agreement has two initially unchecked choices: Optional — Share usage data and Required — I agree to the Terms of Use. Accepting the Terms enables Continue even if sharing remains unchecked. You can use the app and purchase Premium without analytics. The sheet links to this Privacy Policy and the applicable Apple standard EULA. Continue saves the Terms acceptance and the separate sharing choice. Acceptance of the older single-button agreement is not treated as a separate analytics choice: after updating, those users see the new unchecked choices, and the SDK remains inactive until sharing is explicitly selected. The new acceptance and sharing preference are saved across launches and updates. To change your choice, open Alarms, go to Settings and use Share usage data under Analytics. Turning it off stops SDK data sending, saves the preference and prevents SDK activation on subsequent launches while it is off. Turning it off does not delete data already sent to the provider; it is not a request to erase that data or the SDK's local cache.

AppMetrica processes analytics data on our behalf. Its documentation states that data may be stored and processed in the European Union and the Russian Federation. See AppMetrica's data security information and Yandex's privacy policy for provider practices. For privacy questions or a data request, contact us below. Because Alarms has no user account, identifying a particular analytics record may require additional information.

Information stored on your device

Your alarm plans (time, repeat days, label, sound, missions and registered QR payload), morning log (alarm and completion times, mission and outcome) and settings are stored in Alarms on your iPhone. The widget reads the next alarm from storage shared with the app on the same device. Apple device backups may include app data. We do not operate a server that stores or synchronizes these alarm plans or logs.

Data retention

On your device: alarm plans, morning history and preferences remain in the app's storage until you delete the relevant data or delete the app. Deleting an alarm does not delete its existing morning-history entries. The free plan's seven-day history view is a display limit, not an automatic deletion period: older entries remain stored for Premium access. This version has no separate control for clearing all morning history. To remove the app's local database, settings and SDK cache, use Delete App in iPhone Settings, rather than Offload App, which keeps app data. Copies in device backups are managed separately through Apple's backup settings; restoring a backup may restore app data. System-managed identifiers, including identifiers stored by the SDK in the iOS Keychain, may remain after the app is deleted.

Analytics held by AppMetrica: Alarms does not currently configure a fixed automatic expiry period for these records. Retention is tied to the period during which our Alarms app record remains active in AppMetrica, subject to deletion requests and the provider's processing agreement. The criteria for continued retention are whether the records are needed to assess feature usage, compare app versions and understand alarm, mission and purchase flows, together with any applicable legal obligations. Records are subject to deletion when those purposes no longer require them, when a valid deletion request can be matched to them, or when our use of the service ends. The provider may also delete records for technical reasons. The provider's Data Processing Agreement addresses deletion at the end of processing and exceptions where continued storage is required by law. Turning off sharing or uninstalling Alarms does not itself erase records already held by AppMetrica.

Privacy and support correspondence: if you email us, we receive your email address, the message and any attachments you choose to send. We use that information to answer the request and keep it for as long as needed to resolve the request, handle related follow-up and meet applicable legal obligations. Retention depends on whether the request is still open, whether follow-up is needed and whether a legal requirement applies. You can include this correspondence in a deletion request. App Store payment and subscription records are controlled by Apple under Apple's privacy policy; deleting Alarms does not cancel a subscription or delete Apple's records.

Requesting deletion of data

To request deletion of analytics data or privacy/support correspondence, email rietttube@gmail.com with the subject Alarms privacy request. State what you want deleted and provide the app version, device model and approximate dates of use if available. First turn off Settings > Analytics > Share usage data in Alarms to stop new SDK data sending while the request is handled. Disabling sharing and requesting deletion are separate actions.

We review the request, ask only for additional information reasonably necessary to identify the relevant records and verify the request, and use AppMetrica's available deletion tools or contact its support to arrange deletion. Alarms has no account, does not send your email address as an analytics identifier, and this version does not display the SDK's installation identifier. Your email address, device model and dates alone may therefore be insufficient to locate a particular installation. If we cannot reliably associate a request with the records, we explain that limitation and the available next steps rather than claim that identifiable records have been deleted. Do not send passwords, Apple Account credentials or payment-card details.

We respond without undue delay and within the time required by applicable law. We explain any necessary additional verification, permitted extension or legal reason for retaining particular information, and report the outcome of the request. A response deadline is not a promise that the provider's deletion process has already completed. AppMetrica documents a 14-day recovery window after the app owner deletes app records through its service; after that window those records cannot be restored through that process. This window starts with provider-side deletion, not with uninstalling Alarms or emailing us. See AppMetrica's deletion documentation. Information that has been irreversibly aggregated and cannot be associated with an installation may remain in statistical reports.

Purchases and subscriptions

Premium is sold through the App Store. Alarms uses StoreKit to load products, make purchases, restore purchases and check subscription entitlements. Apple handles the payment and subscription account. The analytics purchase metadata described above is separate from payment processing.

Permissions

You can change permissions in the iPhone Settings app.

Alarms allows system alarms to ring through Silent mode and Focus with the app closed.

Motion & Fitness supports movement missions. Motion and step readings are used for the mission and are not sent as analytics events.

Camera is used to register and scan a QR code. The app does not save or send camera images.

Notifications supports the optional wake-up check, a reminder five minutes after a mission.

Children

Alarms is not directed at children under 13. It does not ask for a user's age, so we cannot determine the age of every user. Analytics can process the technical and usage data described above whenever it is enabled, including on a device used by a child. A parent or guardian can disable Share usage data in Settings and contact us with privacy questions.
